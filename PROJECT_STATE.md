## 2. Mass & Weight Reality (As-Built vs. Design)

> [!NOTE]
> **Mass & Flight Baseline**:
> - **Design Target MTOW (Original Concept)**: `3.448 kg (33.8 N)`
> - **Operational As-Built Ceiling**: **`5.687 kg (55.8 N)`** (Option A motors + 2S pack baseline).
> - **Builder As-Built Target**: **`< 5.60 kg`** (Conservative Baseline: `4.813 kg / 47.2 N`; Task-Optimized Servos: `4.489 kg / 44.0 N`; Ultra-Light Build: `3.67 kg / 36.0 N` with full fairing kit).
> - **Physical Wing Geometry & Area Calibration**: Each wing comprises 1 root mount + 4 modular 250 mm segments + wingtip ($b/2 = 1,150.0\text{ mm}$, tip-to-tip span $b = 2.348\text{ m}$, $\text{MAC} = 143.7\text{ mm}$, root chord $165.0\text{ mm}$, $AR = 18.0$). Total projected lifting area across all 8 wings is **$S = 1.224\text{ m}^2$** (+71.8% over legacy concept).
> - **Lift Deficit Reversal**: At $9.0\text{ m/s}$ cruise ($q = 49.61\text{ Pa}$, $C_L = 1.05$), static aerodynamic wing lift is **$63.76\text{ N}$**, generating an immediate **$+16.56\text{ N}$ to $+19.74\text{ N}$ passive lift surplus** over aircraft weight. Clean unpowered $1g$ level glide speed drops to **$7.48\text{--}7.74\text{ m/s}$** ($16.7\text{--}17.3\text{ mph}$), with stall speed at **$6.06\text{--}6.27\text{ m/s}$** ($13.6\text{--}14.0\text{ mph}$).
> - **Aerodynamic Fairing Package (`STRUCT-15`, `STRUCT-16`)**: Foaming LW-PLA compact delta fuselage truss infill ($95\text{ g}$) + 8× teardrop wing root nacelles ($48\text{ g}$, $143\text{ g}$ total) with compliant wiper seals. Reduces parasite drag area $f_{\text{par}}$ by **$-49.1\%$** ($0.0550\text{ m}^2 \to \mathbf{0.0280\text{ m}^2}$), saving $88.6\text{ W}$ thrust power ($123.1\text{ W}$ shaft) at $17.5\text{ m/s}$.
> - **High-Speed Envelopes**: Continuous fast cruise speed (`H-FAST-CRUISE-1`) jumps to **$20.80\text{ m/s}$ ($46.5\text{ mph}$)** at $160\text{ W}$ shaft power. Level flapping dash sprint (`H-46`) reaches **$26.35\text{ m/s}$ ($58.9\text{ mph}$)** at $450\text{ W}$ burst.
> - **Longitudinal Balance**: With the 6S main battery and 2S servo battery mounted on the quick-pin sliding tray at **arm = 250 mm**, the Center of Gravity sits at **311.8 mm to 323.8 mm (26.5% MAC)**, directly in the center of the safe envelope (**IN ENVELOPE: YES ✓**).
> - **Dynamic Flight Law Calibration Gate**: Gross weight is the primary post-build calibration figure (`FIG-01`). Flight laws dynamically calculate stall speed $V_s \propto \sqrt{m}$, lift demand $L = mg$, Boyd $P_s$, and hover $T/W$ margin directly from the builder's measured scale entry.
> - **Hover Status**: Retained as an ideal performance goal and bench characterization gate (TC-01/TC-02). At masses below 4.8 kg, static vertical thrust ($48\text{--}53\text{ N}$) provides $T/W \ge 1.05\text{--}1.15$, unlocking zero-speed hover and leap takeoffs.
